

# HMR Landing Page — Implementation Plan

## Overview
A dark, techy single-page website for Hybrid Maglev Railways (HMR) that showcases the team's vision, mission, and technology. The design will use a dark background with blue gradient accents (light-blue → dark-blue) matching the logo, with glowing effects and futuristic typography to appeal to engineering students and sponsors.

---

## Sections

### 1. Hero Section
- Full-screen hero with a bold headline: *"Engineering the Future of Rail"*
- Animated gradient text and subtle particle/glow effects in the background
- Short tagline about bridging historical infrastructure with futuristic propulsion
- CTA buttons: **"Join the Team"** and **"Learn More"** (scrolls down)
- HMR branding with the blue gradient color scheme

### 2. Vision Section
- Three highlight cards with icons:
  - **Frictionless Journey** — silent, smooth travel
  - **Speed Meets Sustainability** — replacing short-haul flights
  - **Infrastructure as an Asset** — upgrading existing rail
- Subtle scroll animations as cards enter view

### 3. Mission Section
- Concise mission statement with key goals presented as an engaging list/timeline:
  - Eliminate friction → lower energy & maintenance
  - Seamless integration with European infrastructure
  - Prove viability through prototyping at TU/e
- Stats or key figures displayed prominently (e.g., "0 Rolling Resistance", "Silent Operation")

### 4. Technology Section
- Three subsections with visual breakdowns:
  - **Electromagnetic Levitation** — how the pods "fly" above tracks
  - **Backward Compatibility** — dual-mode capability & infrastructure overlay
  - **Engineering Process** — multidisciplinary team at TU/e's innovation hub
- Visual diagrams or illustrated icons for each concept
- Glowing blue accent lines connecting the subsections

### 5. Join Us / CTA Section
- Call-to-action targeting engineering students at TU/e
- Highlight disciplines needed: Mechanical, Electrical, Applied Physics
- Contact form or link to application/email
- Secondary CTA for sponsors: *"Partner with us"*

### 6. Footer
- Links to social media, TU/e affiliation
- Contact email
- HMR branding

---

## Design Details
- **Color palette**: Dark background (#0a0a1a), light-blue to dark-blue gradient accents
- **Typography**: Bold, modern sans-serif headings with lighter body text
- **Effects**: Subtle glow effects, smooth scroll animations, gradient borders on cards
- **Responsive**: Fully mobile-friendly layout
- **Navigation**: Sticky top navbar with smooth scroll to each section
